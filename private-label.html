The label pack, per market

Label law is where small brands lose their first shipment. These are the fields we complete for you, and the ones only you can supply.

  • 01Name of the food and true name. A fanciful name alone is not compliant in the EU. It has to sit with the real product name, so the tin reads House Name and then Zhangping Shuixian.
  • 02Net quantity. Metric, in grams, in the statutory field of vision.
  • 03Food business operator. A name and address established in the EU, or in the UK for the UK market. This is the field a Hong Kong only shipper cannot fill.
  • 04Country of origin. China, stated. Required and checked.
  • 05Best before and storage. Date plus the condition, for example store sealed away from light and odour.
  • 06Lot mark. Ties the tin to the retained sample and the residue report.
  • 07Language. The language of the market where it sells, not English by default.
  • 08Nutrition and claims. Tea with no added ingredient is exempt from the nutrition table. No health claim goes on the tin unless it is authorised.
