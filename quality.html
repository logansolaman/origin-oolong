Every claim on this site has a document behind it.

Tea is the easiest food category to oversell and the hardest to verify. So here is the register: what we assert, which paper proves it, and how you check it without taking our word for anything.

Per lot, not per farm.

A residue result belongs to a lot. A certificate that says the farm is clean tells a buyer nothing about the carton in front of them, because the difference between two fields, or two weeks of picking, is bigger than the difference between two farms.

So the panel is run on the lot that will ship, before it is packed rather than after. If a lot fails, it does not get relabelled or blended down. It gets sold domestically and it never appears on this site.

Panel scope: the EU maximum residue list for camellia sinensis, including compounds that are not registered for tea and therefore appear as detections at trace level. Third party verification is welcome on any order and we will hold the lot while yours runs.

Farm assurance claims

If a farm assurance certificate is claimed for a lot, the thirteen digit producer number is quoted so you can check it in the scheme's own supply chain portal. A PDF of a certificate proves that someone once passed an audit. A live registration number proves the producer is in the scheme today, in the right scope, at the right version. Those are different things and buyers should insist on the second.

Retained samples

Every lot keeps a sealed 250 g reference here, dated and co-signed, for twenty four months. It costs almost nothing and it is the only mechanism that resolves a bulk quality dispute without a lawyer.

What we do not claim.

The credibility of the rest of this page depends on this section being honest, so it is the part we would rather not write.

  • 01Organic, unless certified for your market. Good farm practice is not organic certification. If you need organic, we say whether the paperwork exists in your jurisdiction, and we do not imply it otherwise.
  • 02Health claims. Tea is a food. Nothing on this site is a health claim and no tin we print will carry one unless it is authorised in that market.
  • 03Single test marketing. One clean report from three years ago is not a testing programme and we do not present it as one.
  • 04Annual consistency we have not yet proven. We have a fixed recipe: same cultivar, same pick window, same oxidation target, same roast curve. That is the mechanism for consistency. It becomes a track record only after several seasons, and until then we call it a method, not a guarantee.
  • 05Other origins. We do not sell Wuyi, Anxi or Taiwanese tea as our own. If a customer asks for a region we do not supply, we say so and, if we can, we name someone who does.
