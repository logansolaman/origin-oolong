Privacy notice.

Short version: we hold the details you send us in order to answer your enquiry and to run the resulting order. Nothing else.

What we collect

If you email us or submit the sample request form, we receive your name, company, work email, market, expected volume and whatever you write in the message. If we quote you, we add your delivery address, purchase order reference and the shipping documents for that order.

Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry, to prepare a quotation, to ship an order and to keep the commercial record that export, tax and food safety law requires us to keep. We do not sell it, we do not rent it and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Who else sees it

Our freight forwarder, the courier carrying your samples, and the laboratory that tests the lot you buy, and only the details each of them needs. If your market requires a named importer of record, your details go to that party because the label law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become orders are deleted after 24 months. Order records are kept for seven years, which is the retention period our accountants and the export authorities require.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google when the page loads.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to trade@originoolong.com and we will answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or the UK and you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Who we are

Origin Oolong LLC, Hong Kong. Contact for any privacy question: trade@originoolong.com.
