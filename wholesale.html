What a wholesale order actually contains

Leaf is packed in sealed 5 kg foil-lined cartons, one lot per carton, with the lot code printed on the outside and repeated on the invoice. You get the same tea in carton twenty that you got in carton one, because it is the same lot, not the same product name.

The lot pack travels by email before you pay: spec sheet, process record, physical analysis, residue panel and the export filings. A sealed reference sample is held here for twenty four months, co-signed, in case anyone ever disputes whether the bulk matched.

What we will not do

Blend two lots to hit a price. Substitute an unsold lot when stock runs short. Quote a commodity grade we cannot document. If the answer is no, you get it in the first email rather than in the third container.

How an order runs.

  • 01You tell us the use case. Gongfu retail, western infusion, or extraction for beverage. Same leaf passes or fails those three tests differently, and we will not recommend a lot before we know which one you are running.
  • 02Sample kit out. Five lots, 20 g each, with the spec sheet and the residue report. Courier, at cost, refunded against your first order.
  • 03You cup it and pick a lot. Most buyers take two or three weeks here. We hold the lot for 30 days from dispatch of the kit so it does not sell underneath you.
  • 04Quotation against the lot code. Price, Incoterm, lead time, payment terms and arbitration seat on one page. Valid thirty days.
  • 05Packing, documents, shipment. Photographs of the actual cartons, weights and pallet marks before the container closes, and the full document set against the bill of lading.

Payment

50 percent with the order, balance against the bill of lading. Letter of credit at sight on a first order above 500 kg. Prices in USD, EUR or GBP.
