Documentation

The lot pack, in eight certificates.

Oolong has no commodity benchmark. A buyer cannot check a price against an index, so the only thing that makes it a reorderable SKU is the paperwork. Each card names the document and the spec it certifies.

  • Lot codeFarm, cultivar, year, sequence. Printed on every carton, quoted on every invoice.
  • Product standardGB/T 30357.4-2015 for shuixian, official English title cited.
  • Process recordOxidation percent, roast level and date, resting period, moisture at packing.
  • Physical analysisStem, broken leaf, fannings and dust percent, leaf uniformity.
  • Residue panelMulti-residue screen against EU MRLs, issued against the lot number.
  • Export filingsProducer and plantation registrations held with Chinese customs.
  • Retained sampleSealed, dated, co-signed, held 24 months for third-party comparison.
  • TermsIncoterm, lead time, payment terms and arbitration seat on one page.

Provenance

The place, and why the lots are small.

Zhangping sits inland in Fujian, in Longyan prefecture, around 113 kilometres from Xiamen. It is not on the standard oolong map that begins with Wuyi and ends with the Taiwanese high mountains. That omission is the opportunity: no incumbent owns the name in English, and the tea is good enough to be compared with both.

Yongfu, the town we buy from, is a valley of small holdings rather than one estate, which is why lot level documentation matters more here than a farm certificate.

Questions buyers ask first.

What is the minimum order?

Ten kilograms for wholesale leaf. Five hundred units for a private label run in our stock tin. Custom printed tins carry a 3,000 unit minimum, which is the printer's run length, not ours.

Can you supply a lot-matched residue report?

Yes. Every lot offered for EU or UK sale is screened against a multi-residue panel before it is packed, and the report is issued against that lot number rather than against the farm in general.

Do you ship to the EU and the UK?

Yes. Oolong enters the EU at zero percent most-favoured-nation duty under heading 0902. We ship FOB Xiamen or delivered duty paid to an EU or UK warehouse, and we can name an importer of record so your label carries a European food business address.

How do I taste before committing?

A kit of five 20 gram lots goes out by courier with the spec sheet and residue report for each. It is charged at cost and refunded against your first order. Brew it the way your customer will brew it, not the way we do.
