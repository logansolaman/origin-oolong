What arrives with a lot.

Oolong has no commodity benchmark and no ISO chemical standard. A buyer cannot check a price against an index, so the only thing that makes it a reorderable SKU is the paperwork. This is the packet, sent as one PDF before you commit.

Built to clear a European buyer's first five questions.

Most China-direct oolong fails on paperwork before anyone cups it. These are the five things we can answer in one email.

  • 01Can you export legally from China? Producer and raw-material plantation registrations held with the customs authority. Copies go out with the lot pack.
  • 02Will the tea be the same in twelve months? Fixed cultivar, fixed pick window, fixed oxidation target, fixed roast curve. Same recipe, next season.
  • 03Is the residue result repeatable? Tested per lot, not once for marketing. The report carries the lot number.
  • 04Can a buyer enforce a contract? Hong Kong arbitration, English law on the sale, named seat in the terms.
  • 05Will bulk match the sample? A sealed reference sample per lot, co-signed and held, is the only answer that survives a dispute.

Questions buyers ask first.

What is the minimum order?

Ten kilograms for wholesale leaf. Five hundred units for a private label run in our stock tin. Custom printed tins carry a 3,000 unit minimum, which is the printer's run length, not ours.

Can you supply a lot-matched residue report?

Yes. Every lot offered for EU or UK sale is screened against a multi-residue panel before it is packed, and the report is issued against that lot number rather than against the farm in general.

Do you ship to the EU and the UK?

Yes. Oolong enters the EU at zero percent most-favoured-nation duty under heading 0902. We ship FOB Xiamen or delivered duty paid to an EU or UK warehouse, and we can name an importer of record so your label carries a European food business address.

How do I taste before committing?

A kit of five 20 gram lots goes out by courier with the spec sheet and residue report for each. It is charged at cost and refunded against your first order. Brew it the way your customer will brew it, not the way we do.
